What’s simulated

  • Sinking rings. Plastic slightly denser than water, so they fall at about 5 cm/s and turn flat as they go, fluttering on the way down.
  • The pump stroke. A quick tap gives a short, weak puff. Holding pushes the full stroke and a strong jet. Letting go draws a little water back in.
  • Live water. A fluid solver runs in the tank, so each jet curls into eddies that keep carrying rings after the press ends.
  • Real hooking. Each ring is a 3D torus. It only counts when a peg runs through its hole, so rings can glance off the tip or get blasted back off.
